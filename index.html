?How it works / FAQ

Choose 3 activities each day and enter what you actually did: reps, minutes, seconds, or stairs.

Break activities into whatever sets work for you. Your daily total counts, even if you spread it throughout the day.

Each activity counts up to 100 points. If it is today's double-points activity, it can count up to 200. The daily max is 400.

100-point targets Push ups: 50 reps Pull ups: 10 reps Squats: 100 reps Sit ups: 100 reps Plank: 5 minutes Walking: 50 minutes Running: 50 minutes Dancing: 25 minutes Stairs: 500 stairs Bird dog: 50 right-left pairs

The group goal is based on 250 points per person per day. People can see who joined and the team totals, but not each person's daily score.

Challenge days follow Pacific time. The 400 club celebrates people who reached the daily max yesterday.

Push ups may be wall pushups, knee pushups, or full pushups. Sit ups may be full sit ups or crunches. One bird dog rep means a right-left pair.
